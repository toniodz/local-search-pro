02 · Logo

Logo variations

The symbol is a yellow circle with an upward arrow that breaks through its base, standing for growth and better search visibility. The wordmark is set in Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold. Use the primary horizontal logo wherever there is room for it.

Variations 5 and 6 (reversed and dark) are the on-dark and on-light versions of every lockup above. On light backgrounds, "Pro" uses Cyan Deep #0590CC and the full stop becomes navy, because yellow can't be seen on white.

06 · Graphics & icons

Map, arrow and curve graphics

Icon principles

Icons are drawn on a 24px grid with a 2px stroke, round caps and joins, and 2px corner radius. Use outlines only, with no fills. Use cyan on navy and navy on light backgrounds. Where an icon sits in a tile, the tile corner radius is 14px. The full icon set comes in Stage 3.

Show Google trademarks only as third-party marks, following Google's own brand rules. They are never part of the Local Search Pro identity.

07 · Imagery

Photography and illustration

Photography: real UK small businesses at work, such as vans, shopfronts, salons, gardens and tradespeople on site. Use natural light and real places. Avoid staged boardrooms, handshakes and generic tech stock.

Device mockups: show clean, flat laptop and phone frames with example websites for generic businesses ("Your Business"). Never show a real client's site without permission.

Illustration: flat shapes in the brand palette, with no gradients or 3D effects. Search and map graphics must use example data, with no fake rankings or star ratings.

Claims: never promise "Number 1 on Google" or "Guaranteed leads".

09 · Accessibility

Contrast recommendations

Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text (24px+, or 19px+ bold) and interface elements.

{{ p.ratio }} {{ p.label }} {{ p.verdict }}

Never use yellow or Electric Cyan for text on white. Don't rely on colour alone to show meaning, so pair status colours with an icon or label. Keep tap targets at least 44px and body text at least 16px.
