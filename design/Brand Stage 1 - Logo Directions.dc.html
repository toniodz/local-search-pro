Each one keeps the yellow symbol and upward arrow. Colours follow the brief: Deep Navy #10283F, Bright Yellow #FFE500, Electric Cyan #05B9F2. Pick one and I'll build the full logo system from it.

The arrow runs right through the circle, from bottom to top, and splits it in two. It reads as breaking through and rising above the competition.

Note: Electric Cyan #05B9F2 is too light on white for small text (about 2.3:1 contrast). Light-background versions use a deeper cyan, #0590CC, for "Pro". This is a proposed refinement to confirm in Stage 2.
