What Is Local SEO?

Local search engine optimisation, or local SEO, is the process of improving how your business appears when people search online for services in a particular area.

For example, potential customers might search for:

  • Electrician in Canterbury
  • Emergency plumber near me
  • Roof repairs in Dover
  • Cleaning company in Folkestone
  • Landscaper in Maidstone

Google uses various signals to decide which businesses and websites appear for these searches.

Local SEO helps make your website and business information more relevant, useful and accessible to people searching for your services.

The goal isn't simply to attract more website visitors. It's to help your business become more visible to people looking for what you offer.
