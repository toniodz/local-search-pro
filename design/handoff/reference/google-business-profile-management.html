Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Google Business Profile?

It's a free business listing that allows eligible businesses to manage how they appear on Google Search and Google Maps.

Can you create a Google Business Profile for me?

We can advise on profile creation and eligibility. New profile creation and verification assistance can be quoted separately where required.

Will this get my business to number one on Google?

No legitimate provider can guarantee a particular Google ranking. Local visibility depends on several factors, including relevance, distance and prominence.

Our service focuses on keeping your profile accurate, complete and professionally maintained.

Can I still access my Google Business Profile?

Yes. You remain in control of your business profile. We request manager access to carry out the agreed work.

Can you remove negative reviews?

Businesses generally cannot remove genuine negative reviews simply because they disagree with them. Reviews that violate Google's policies can be reported to Google.

Is Google Business Profile management the same as SEO?

Not quite. Google Business Profile management focuses on your listing, while local SEO also considers your website, content, search performance and other visibility factors.
