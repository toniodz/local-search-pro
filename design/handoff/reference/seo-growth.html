It's about creating useful pages, helping visitors find the right information and making it easier for potential customers to take the next step.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does SEO Growth cost?

SEO Growth costs £249 per month plus VAT, with a one-off setup fee of £199 plus VAT.

What's the difference between Local SEO and SEO Growth?

Local SEO Essentials focuses on foundational website improvements and Google Business Profile optimisation. SEO Growth includes more time for search performance analysis and additional monthly website improvements.

Can SEO guarantee more customers?

No. Search visibility, traffic and enquiries can be influenced by many factors, including competition, customer demand and your website's ability to convert visitors into enquiries.

Will you create new pages for my website?

Our standard package focuses on optimising existing website content. Where a new page would be beneficial, we can recommend it and provide a separate quotation.

Do you build backlinks?

External link-building campaigns are not included in this package. We prioritise website content, technical foundations and relevant on-page improvements.

How will I know what you're doing each month?

You'll receive a monthly summary of the work carried out, relevant performance indicators and the recommended next steps.

Can you work with WordPress websites?

Yes, we can work with many WordPress websites and other platforms, subject to a technical review and the necessary access.
