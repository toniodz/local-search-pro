Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a pay monthly website cost?

Our Website Starter package costs £79 per month plus VAT, with a £149 initial setup fee plus VAT. Additional pages and features are available at an extra cost.

Is there a minimum contract?

Yes. Website Starter is based on a 24-month minimum agreement. Your contract will explain the payment terms, cancellation process and options for your website at the end of the agreement.

Can I use my existing domain name?

Yes. If you already have a domain name, we can normally connect it to your new website, provided you have the necessary access.

Will my website appear on Google?

Your website will include basic SEO setup to help search engines discover and understand its pages. However, appearing prominently for competitive searches usually requires additional optimisation and ongoing work.

Can I add more pages later?

Yes. Additional pages and features can be quoted separately as your business grows.

Can you update my website?

Limited content amendments are included within your package. Larger changes, additional functionality and redesigns can be quoted separately.

What happens if I cancel?

Your agreement will set out cancellation terms, ownership and any options to purchase or transfer your website. We will explain these before you sign up.
