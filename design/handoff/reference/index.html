Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a small business website cost?

Our pay-monthly websites start from £79 per month plus VAT, with an initial setup fee. This includes your website, hosting and ongoing maintenance within the agreed package.

Do I need a website for local SEO?

Not necessarily. You can start with Google Business Profile management, although a well-optimised website can provide more opportunities to attract customers through search.

Can you improve my existing website's SEO?

Yes. Our local SEO packages can work with existing websites, subject to an initial review of your website and its platform.

How long does local SEO take to work?

SEO is an ongoing process, and results depend on your industry, competition, location and existing online presence. Some improvements may become visible relatively quickly, while more competitive searches can take several months or longer. Rankings and enquiries cannot be guaranteed.

Can I upgrade my package?

Yes. As your business grows, you can move to a package that includes more ongoing support and optimisation.

Do you work with businesses across the UK?

Yes. Local Search Pro offers website and SEO services to small businesses throughout the UK.
